Privacy

Last updated 31 July 2026 · Research preview

The current public site does not accept resume uploads or create personal career profiles. This notice also describes the controls planned before those features are released.

What we collect

The public preview may receive ordinary technical request data needed to deliver and secure the site. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include. Before Career Match launches, we will provide a point-of-collection notice describing any account, resume and derived profile data.

How resume data will be handled

  • Upload will be voluntary and used to provide the requested analysis.
  • Access will be restricted and data will be protected in transit and at rest.
  • Users will be able to delete the original resume and derived profile.
  • Personal resume data will not be sold or shared with employers for advertising or screening.
  • We will collect only the information needed for the stated service and publish a retention period before launch.

Your choices

Depending on where you live and whether applicable legal thresholds are met, you may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information, and to opt out of certain sale, sharing or profiling. We intend to honor verified access, correction and deletion requests for all users where feasible. A dedicated privacy request channel will be added before personal-data features launch.

Current references

Our planned controls follow the FTC’s guidance to inventory, minimize, protect and dispose of personal information, and recognize the privacy rights described by the California Attorney General. See the FTC business guide and the California CCPA overview.

Contact

A verified privacy-request contact and request workflow will be published before PM Signal begins collecting account or resume data.
