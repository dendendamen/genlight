Methodology

PM Market Index framework · Shadow period

What PM Signal measures

PM Signal measures externally published US product-management openings in a fixed employer panel. AI PM is a subset of the full PM denominator. The panel is intentionally observable and reproducible; it is not a census of every US employer.

Current sources

We collect published postings from documented Greenhouse Job Board and Lever Postings interfaces. Raw responses, retrieval timestamps, parsers and record hashes are preserved. Sources that fail do not silently contribute stale observations.

Classification

PM classification requires a qualifying role title and product ownership evidence. AI PM additionally requires explicit AI product scope. Detailed classification separates product family, customer, product layer and business motion. Low-confidence labels enter review rather than being hidden.

Index publication gate

The PM Market Index combines posting stock, new-posting flow and employer breadth. The AI PM Trend Index measures AI share, AI employer breadth and share of new PM openings. Scores remain unpublished until 28 complete daily observations pass quality gates.

Known limitations

  • The fixed panel over-represents technology and AI-active employers.
  • Several large employers use unsupported career systems and are absent.
  • Posted compensation coverage varies by employer and location.
  • Job descriptions can be stale, duplicated or removed without a hire.
  • Classification describes observable role scope, not the quality of the employer or candidate.
