Informational service

PM Signal provides market research and career decision support. It does not provide legal, financial, recruiting or employment advice. Index values, classifications and personal matching outputs may contain errors and do not guarantee interviews, offers, compensation or career outcomes.

Not an employment decision tool

PM Signal is designed for individuals to understand the market. It may not be used to automatically screen, rank, reject or make employment decisions about candidates.

Employment tests and selection procedures can create discrimination risks and may require validation, notice and bias auditing. Employers remain responsible for their own compliance. See the EEOC selection-procedure guidance and New York City’s AEDT requirements.

Data and third-party sources

Market observations are derived from externally published job postings available through documented public job-board interfaces. PM Signal is independent and is not endorsed by or affiliated with the employers or job-board providers represented in the data. Source links and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Acceptable use

You may use public aggregate findings for personal and internal research with attribution. You may not circumvent access controls, repackage paid data, misuse personal information, or use the service to discriminate or make unlawful employment decisions.

Changes and availability

This is an early research preview. Coverage, classifications and features may change, and the service may be interrupted. Material methodology changes will be versioned and disclosed.
